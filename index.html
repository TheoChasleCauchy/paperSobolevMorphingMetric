Reference Timbre Space

Parameters

  • log(ω): fundamental frequency of the drum, controlling pitch
  • 𝜏: duration of the drum sound, inversely proportional to the modulation frequency
  • log(p): frequency-dependent damping coefficient in log-scale, allowing to control the material of the plate
  • log(D): dispersion parameter, controlling the inharmonicity of the overtones in log-scale
  • α: aspect ratio of the rectangular drum membrane

Listening to the extrema of the hypercube

In the defined hypercube, the synthesizer produces a metal-like sound for log(p) at its minimum value and a wooden-like sound for log(p) at its maximum value.
